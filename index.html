About Wayne

A training philosophy built on lifestyle change, not shortcuts.

I believe that improved health and fitness come as a result of adopting a healthy lifestyle. Combining exercise with proper nutrition is the way to achieve your goals.

Helping people reach their goals has always been important to me. I became ACE certified in order to provide you the skills, motivation, and education you need to get there.

If you're ready to make the commitment to yourself, contact me and let's get started.
